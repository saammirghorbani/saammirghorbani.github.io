Referral Program Funnel Analysis

2026
Acquisition · Funnel analysis · Concentration analysis

Referrals were a candidate channel for lower-CAC growth, but the loop had never been pressure-tested. Decomposed it into a MECE funnel, quantified each stage with conversion rates and confidence intervals, and used Lorenz-curve concentration analysis to surface where the value actually lives. Then sized the addressable opportunity and recommended the first experiment.

Largeuntapped long-tenure subscriber pool identified as a CRM target
Gini 0.97revenue concentration revealing a power-law referrer distribution
Awarenessidentified as the dominant lever for free users
Approach
  • MECE funnel decomposition (awareness, join, copy link, click, signup, pay) with conversion rates and 95% confidence intervals at every stage.
  • Subscriber versus non-subscriber funnel comparison: surfaced the counterintuitive finding that non-subscriber clicks convert at similar rates to subscribers, but reach is far smaller, so the gap is awareness rather than conversion quality.
  • Lorenz-curve and Gini-coefficient concentration analysis on revenue (Gini 0.97) and clicks (Gini 0.87), establishing the power-law structure of referrer value.
  • Tenure, plan and country-tier segmentation identified the highest-yield CRM pool.
  • P90-based experiment-window calculation across the funnel to set the right read points given the long maturation cycle.
Outcome
  • Identified low awareness as the dominant lever, not conversion quality.
  • Surfaced a large untapped pool of long-tenure subscribers as a high-yield CRM target.
  • Recommended a global awareness test for free users as the first experiment, with P90-anchored read points for early metrics and full end-to-end evaluation.

ML for Fleet Fuel Reduction

2023 – 2024
Machine learning · Explainability · Customer-facing product

Fuel is one of the largest operating costs in commercial fleets, and a meaningful share of that cost is driven by behaviours only visible in high-frequency sensor data. Built XGBoost regression models on truck-sensor telemetry to predict fuel consumption from driver behaviour, then used SHAP-based feature importance to translate the model into a driver-facing product that nudges drivers toward the patterns the data showed actually move the needle.

Up to 5%fuel-consumption reduction at fleet level
XGBoost + SHAPregression with interpretability built in
Customer-facingshipped inside a driver-facing product
Approach
  • Feature engineering on truck-sensor telemetry data: aggregated into driver-behaviour signals at the trip and segment level.
  • XGBoost regression to predict fuel consumption from driver-behaviour features.
  • SHAP-based feature importance to identify the behaviours most strongly tied to higher consumption and translate them into actionable product nudges.
  • Partnered with product and analytics teams to shape how the model’s insights surface inside the driver-facing application.
Outcome
  • Insights shaped the design of a driver-facing product rated for up to 5% fuel-consumption reduction at fleet level, depending on adoption and usage.
