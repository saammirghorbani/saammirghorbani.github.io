Referral Program Funnel Analysis

2025 – 2026
Marketing & acquisition · Funnel analysis

Referrals were a candidate channel for lower-CAC growth, but the loop had never been pressure-tested. Decomposed it into a MECE funnel (awareness, join, copy link, click, signup, pay), quantified each stage with conversion rates and confidence intervals, and segmented referrers so the team could see where reach and downstream conversion were actually coming from.

Awareness-to-joinidentified as the largest drop-off
Global CTAtest design replaced targeted nudges
Early-readmetric defined for a long maturation cycle
Approach
  • Funnel decomposition with conversion rates and 95% CIs at every stage, sliced by cohort month and user segment.
  • Compared subscribers and non-subscribers as referrer types, including reach, click-through, and downstream conversion of referred users.
  • Distribution analysis of referrals per active referrer to test for power-law concentration.
Outcome
  • Identified awareness-to-join as the largest, most addressable drop-off and reframed the program around a global CTA test rather than targeted in-product nudges.
  • Quantified a long maturation cycle, giving the team the right early-read metric to use during experimentation.
  • Findings shaped the direction of the program and the design of the first awareness experiment.
